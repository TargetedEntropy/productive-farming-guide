# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

> Every fact below was inferred from the owner's written brief and from the mod and pack files; no interview was held. Treat entries as assumptions until the owner confirms them.

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: plain static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript with no build step, because the deploy target is GitHub Pages and the content is a fixed dataset generated once from the mod jar.

## Users

Players of the All the Mods 11 modpack (Minecraft 26.1.2, NeoForge) who have just met Productive Farming in the quest book, or who already farm with it and want exact numbers. They read with the game open in another window, often mid-task: "what do I cross to get Rainbow Corn", "which villager level sells grapes", "why is my controller invalid".

## Product Purpose

An unofficial field guide to Productive Farming as it ships in ATM11. It explains how the mod works, lists every crop, flower, mutation, trade and recipe in a searchable form, and collects the high-leverage details that the in-game quest book leaves out. Success is a player answering their question in under a minute without alt-tabbing through JEI.

## Positioning

Everything on the page is read from the shipped jar (productivefarming-26.1.2-0.12.1) and the ATM11 0.11.1-beta pack files, with mechanics checked against the mod's public source. Where the quest book and the code disagree, the guide says so.

## Operating Context

Read on a second monitor or a phone beside the game. Sessions are short and question-driven; a few readers will go top to bottom once. In game, players confirm details through JEI, Jade tooltips and Ponder.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site on GitHub Pages; no backend, no tracking, works offline once loaded.
- The mod is All Rights Reserved: no mod textures, models or code are redistributed. Names and numbers only; visuals are authored for this site.
- API keys and local reference notes used during data collection never enter the repository.
- Data reflects one mod version and one pack version, both shown on the page.

## Brand Commitments

Unofficial and fan-made; not affiliated with the mod author, the ATM team, CurseForge or Mojang. Credit and links to the original download pages are mandatory.

## Evidence on Hand

- `assets/data.js`, generated by `tools/build_data.py` from the unpacked jar and pack.
- Mechanics constants from the mod's source repository (JDKDigital/productivefarming, branch dev-26.1) and its issue tracker.
- ATM11 quest chapter text for Productive Farming.
- No screenshots, no testimonials, no playtest measurements: none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Numbers come from the files, and the page says which file.
2. Answer the question first; explain second.
3. Flag uncertainty and upstream bugs plainly instead of smoothing them over.
4. Credit the people who made the mod and the pack, prominently.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, full keyboard operation of search and filters, reduced-motion respected, usable at 360px wide.
