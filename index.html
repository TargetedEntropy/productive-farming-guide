Productive Farming in All the Mods 11

Productive Farming adds 168 crops that carry four inheritable traits, 19 crosses that bees make for you, 35 dye-bearing flowers and a multiblock farm that harvests on its own. This guide reads its numbers from the shipped jar and the ATM11 pack files, and marks the places where the quest book and the code disagree.

A

Start here

You do not craft your way into this mod. Seeds come from farmer villagers and from village farm plots, and everything else follows from planting them. In ATM11 the quest chapter is called Productive Farming and sits in the same group as the other Productive mods.

  1. Walk a village first

    About a third of the wheat in village farm plots is swapped for mod crops when the village generates. Plains villages can hold fifteen kinds, from Bok Choy to Spring Onion. Break them and you have seeds before you own an emerald.

  2. Trade with a farmer

    A novice farmer can offer any of 97 ground and tall crops at one emerald for four seeds. Higher levels add herbs and berries, then mushrooms, grapes and melons, then trellis crops. Master level adds nothing new.

  3. Plant, then read the tooltip

    Hold Shift over any seed to see its four traits. With Jade installed, as it is in ATM11, looking at a planted crop shows the same traits and any pending mutation.

  4. Harvest with a right-click

    Right-clicking a ripe crop takes the produce and leaves the plant in the ground with its traits intact. Those traits improve a little each time it ripens, so the plant you keep is worth more than the one you replant.

  5. Put a beehive beside two crops

    Any beehive within four blocks of a matching pair can turn one of them into a new variety. That is how you reach the 19 crops no villager sells.

  6. Build the Farm Controller

    A ring of bricks or glass with a controller in the wall becomes a farm that harvests, collects and can fertilise from a fish tank underneath. Section E has the rules.

B

Crop forms

The crops come in eight growth forms. The form decides where a seed can go and what a harvest leaves behind, so it is the first thing to check when something refuses to plant.

Ground crops and grains

Plant on farmland like wheat. Seven growth stages. A right-click harvest sets the plant back to a seedling.

Tall crops

Corn, tomatoes, cotton and friends need two blocks of air. After a harvest they drop back to a mid stage, not to a seedling, so they come back faster.

Water crops

Water Chestnut, Watercress and Water Caltrop go into water one block deep. The lower half sits in the water and the upper half must be in air.

Herbs

Grow like ground crops. Shears on a plant past its second stage take a cutting you can replant and step its growth back one stage.

Berry bushes

Thirteen berries. Picking leaves the bush at a mid stage so it fruits again quickly, the same way sweet berries do.

Fence-post crops

Kiwi, Goji Berry, the four grapes and eight climbers such as Cucumber, Vanilla and Luffa. Set a fence post on farmland and plant into the post. The plant spreads to neighbouring posts.

Melons

Cantaloupe and Honeydew grow on a stem with the fruit block beside it. Nine slices craft a whole melon. One slice crafts back into seeds.

Mushrooms

Feed a mushroom to a composter. One time in five a growth sprouts on a free side of the composter and ripens over five stages. The four vanilla mushrooms and fungi do this too.

C

Traits

Every seed and every planted crop carries four traits. They travel with the seed, they pass to whatever the crop mutates into, and they climb by one step at random as the plant keeps ripening.

The four traits in mod version 0.12.1

YieldLow, Medium, High, Very HighAdds one extra item per level to every stack of produce a harvest drops. Very High is +3. Seeds are not multiplied.
MutabilityNone, Low, Medium, High, Very HighMultiplies the chance of a cross by 1 + 0.5 per level, so Very High triples it.
GrowthLow, Medium, High, Very HighMeant to speed growth. In an AgriTech: Evolved planter it divides the processing time by level plus one. For wheat, Mystical Agriculture and other outside crops it forces extra growth ticks. For the mod's own crops in soil it currently does the opposite. See the notice below.

Known bug: Growth slows the mod's own crops

The mod's crops grow about twice as fast as vanilla wheat at Growth Low. Each Growth level then divides that speed instead of multiplying it, so a Very High plant in soil ripens at roughly a quarter of the pace. This is issue 35 on the mod's tracker, open as of October 2026. Until it is fixed, high-Growth seeds belong in planters, not in the ground.

How traits climb

Each time a crop reaches full ripeness there is a 7% chance that one of the four traits, picked at random, goes up a level. A roll that lands on a trait already at its cap is wasted. On average that is one point every 14 ripenings, about 170 ripenings to reach Very High Yield, and about 320 to max everything.

A right-click harvest, and the Farm Controller, keep the same plant in the ground, so its traits keep accumulating. Breaking the crop drops seeds stamped with the plant's current traits.

Crops from other mods

Vanilla wheat, carrots, potatoes and beetroot gain traits too, along with Mystical Agriculture, Croptopia and the Silent Gear flax and fluffy plants. All of those are in ATM11. Mystical Agriculture seeds climb more slowly the higher their tier.

D

Crossbreeding

A cross needs two crops and a bee. One crop is the target, the plant that changes. The other is the pollen donor. When it works the target is marked with a mutation, and harvesting it gives seeds of the new variety that carry the target's traits.

With a hive

Every time a bee returns to its hive and delivers nectar, the hive scans a cube reaching four blocks in every direction. If both halves of a recipe are inside, it picks one matching recipe at random and rolls its chance against the target. A Productive Bees advanced hive reaches two blocks further per Range upgrade.

Because the hive picks one recipe per visit, a hive surrounded by several possible crosses spreads its attempts across all of them. One hive per cross is faster.

By hand

The Pollen Sieve upgrade, a Productive Bees item this mod adds, gives an advanced hive a 2% chance per bee visit to bottle Pollen from a random crop in range. Use Pollen on a ripe target crop to roll the cross yourself. The Pollen is used up whether or not it takes.

The odds

Chance equals the recipe's base chance times 1 + 0.5 for each Mutability level on the target. A Crop Duster bee from Productive Bees multiplies the result by five, and does the same for the sieve.

Collecting the result

Jade shows the pending mutation on the crop. Right-click a ripe mutated crop to harvest it safely: you get the new seed and the plant returns to normal. Breaking it works as well.

Roma and Beefsteak share three outcomes

Black Beauty, Blue Beauty and White Wonder all come from pollinating Beefsteak with Roma. Each attempt picks one of the three at random before it rolls, so expect to collect them in no particular order.

E

Auto-farm

The Farm Controller turns a walled bed into a machine. It right-click harvests everything on the bed, pulls the drops into its own 27 slots, and if there is a fish tank under the bed it fertilises from that too. It handles this mod's crops, vanilla crops, Mystical Agriculture and the fruit on Productive Trees.

Building rules

  • Walls form one closed rectangle with the controller somewhere in it. The smallest farm is a 3 by 3 ring around a single block.
  • The inside of the ring, at the same height as the top of the wall, is your soil. Crops are harvested in the two blocks above it.
  • Wall blocks are any stone bricks, any glass, hoppers, and the brick blocks listed below. You can mix them.
  • Right-click the controller. It either reports the farm as assembled or names the block that broke the shape.
  • Pipes can pull items from the controller and push Nutrient Water into it.

Size limits

The mod's defaults allow a wall loop of 200 blocks, an inside volume of 1024 blocks and walls 20 blocks deep. ATM11 ships no override for these, which makes a 32 by 32 bed the largest single-layer farm.

The quest book gives different numbers

The ATM11 quest text describes a 2 by 2 minimum and a ceiling of 200 blocks of volume, or a 10 by 10 bed. The mod's own Ponder scene and code say 3 by 3 and 1024. If a large farm is refused, the controller's message prints the volume it measured against the limit in force on your server.

Cycle time

The controller works once every 264 ticks with no upgrades. It has four upgrade slots that take Time, Time II and Stability upgrades from Productive Lib. Each Time upgrade counts once and each Time II counts twice.

Stability upgrade

Produce with different traits does not stack, and a farm of slowly improving plants fills its 27 slots with one-item stacks. A Stability upgrade strips the traits from everything the controller collects, so it stacks again. It strips seeds as well, so keep your breeding bed on a separate controller without one.

Full output

When no slot is free the controller stops harvesting and leaves ripe crops standing. Nothing is destroyed.

Fish farm and Nutrient Water

Build the walls at least three blocks tall and the space under the soil becomes a tank. The controller breeds the fish in it, culls the surplus into drops, and makes Nutrient Water.

  • Each fish adds 50 mB of Nutrient Water per cycle. The controller holds 10,000 mB.
  • Each 50 mB is spent as one bone meal on one growing crop, or on one Productive Trees fruit tree.
  • The tank supports one fish for every two blocks of the farm's footprint. Beyond that the controller culls, always leaving two of each species.
  • Cod, Salmon, Tropical Fish, Pufferfish, Squid and Glow Squid all count, as does any other mod's fish.
  • Nutrient Water can be bucketed out of one controller and into another, or piped.

Claimed chunks block the farm

The controller harvests through a fake player. Inside an FTB Chunks claim it will sit idle or fail to replant until you allow fake players in the claim's settings. This is the most common cause of a controller that forms but does nothing.

F

Animals

Two troughs automate a pasture. Both act once a minute on animals within four blocks sideways and one block up or down, and both take Range and Time upgrades. A Range upgrade adds one block. A Time upgrade shortens the interval by 5%.

Feeding Trough

Nine slots of feed, filled by hand or by pipe. Each minute it offers every nearby adult its breeding food, which puts the animal in love mode exactly as if you had fed it. Any breeding item works, so one trough can hold wheat, seeds and carrots for a mixed pen. A Child upgrade lets it feed babies as well, which speeds their growth.

Watering Trough

Holds ten buckets of water, filled by bucket or pipe. Each minute it takes ten seconds off the breeding cooldown of every adult that is waiting to breed again, at 100 mB per animal. That shortens the five-minute cooldown by about a seventh.

Tooltip and code differ on the Watering Trough

The item tooltip and the quest book say it makes baby animals grow faster. The 0.12 code only acts on adults with a breeding cooldown. Treat it as a breeding-rate block. For faster babies use a Feeding Trough with a Child upgrade.

G

Flowers and dye

The mod adds 18 wild flowers and 3 flowering vines, and gives 14 vanilla flowers a version that can hold any colour. A flower's colour is a real value, and bees mix it.

When a hive has two different kinds of flower in range, each bee visit has a 15% chance to plant a new flower of the first kind nearby. Its colour is a blend of the two parents, somewhere between 30% and 70% of the way from one to the other. Put any coloured flower alone in a crafting grid and you get the dye nearest to its colour. Tall flowers give two.

ATM11 includes Dyenamics, so its eighteen extra dyes are in play. Great Lobelia already gives Ultramarine and Zinnia gives Amber without any breeding.

Blend preview

    Five evenly spaced samples of the range a single cross can land in, with the dye each would craft into.

    Bone meal will probably not spawn them

    The quest book says the new flowers appear when you bone meal grass. That behaviour is behind a config switch which is off by default, and the pack does not turn it on. Expect to find them growing wild in forests, plains, jungles, swamps and hills, at roughly one patch per 32 chunks, then multiply them with bees.

        J

        Power-player points

        The details that change how you build. Each one is drawn from the jar or the source, not from play-testing, so check anything surprising in a test world before you commit a base to it.

        1. Yield on Mystical Agriculture is free essence

          Outside crops gain the same traits, and Yield adds its bonus to every non-seed drop. An Inferium or resource crop at Very High Yield drops three extra essence per harvest. Low-tier seeds improve at 6% per ripening, top-tier ones at 1%.

        2. Grow fast seeds in planters, not soil

          In an AgriTech: Evolved planter a Very High Growth seed finishes in a quarter of the time and Yield still applies. In soil, Growth currently slows this mod's own crops. Seeds left in a planter also keep gaining traits.

        3. Medium Mutability makes the 50% crosses certain

          Beefsteak Tomato and Blue Jade Corn start at 50%. Two levels of Mutability on the target doubles that to a guaranteed result. The 40% corn crosses become certain at High. The 20% crosses top out at 60%.

        4. A Crop Duster bee finishes the job

          Its five-times multiplier turns every 20% cross into a certainty with no Mutability at all, and lifts the Pollen Sieve from 2% to 10% per visit.

        5. One cross per hive

          A hive picks one valid recipe at random per bee visit. Keep unrelated pairs more than four blocks from each other's hive and each hive spends every visit on the cross you want.

        6. Breed on one bed, produce on another

          Keep a small right-click bed for improving traits. Run production beds on controllers with a Stability upgrade so the output stacks. Move seeds from the first to the second when they are good enough.

        7. Three Time II and one Time I reach the speed cap

          That load brings the controller to one cycle a second. If you need a Stability upgrade in the fourth slot, three Time II alone still run a cycle every 2.4 seconds.

        8. Size the fish tank to the bed

          One fish pays for one bone meal on one crop per cycle. A bed at its fish cap, half the footprint, can fertilise about half its crops every cycle, which is far more than most crops need.

        9. Luffa is a renewable sponge

          Smoke Luffa into Dried Luffa and craft nine into a vanilla Sponge. Luffa is an expert-level farmer trade and grows on a fence post.

        10. Loot village farms in several biomes

          Plains plots draw from a list of fifteen crops, savanna, snowy and taiga plots from a list of ten, and desert plots can hold Eggplant, Lentils and Mustard. It is the cheapest way to skip the novice trade lottery.

        11. Compost the mushrooms last

          Mushrooms compost at 65%, crops and herbs at 50%, seeds at 30%. A mushroom thrown in a composter also has its one-in-five chance to sprout a new growth, so a ring of composters is a mushroom farm that feeds itself.

        12. The pack keeps crossbreeds out of the Seed Bag

          ATM11's one config change that touches this mod is in BBL Strainers. Its Seed Bag list has all 19 cross-only crops removed and leaves the villager-sold crops eligible. A Seed Bag can save you a trade. It will never save you a cross.

        13. Crates and seed bags are nine-to-one

          107 kinds of produce pack into crates and 51 kinds of seed into bags, both reversible. Strip traits first or the nine items will not match.

        Where this guide and the quest book part ways

        • Farm size: the book says 2 by 2 up to 10 by 10, the code says 3 by 3 up to a volume of 1024. See section E.
        • Watering Trough: the book says faster babies, the code shortens adult breeding cooldown. See section F.
        • Flowers from bone meal: off by default. See section G.

        Where they differ, trust what you see in your own world, and tell the mod author or the pack team which one was wrong.
